About Masajid Project

The foundational charter, 6-stage project lifecycle, 8 intervention categories, and governance framework guiding our platform.

The Story & Vision

Across urban and rural communities, many masajid suffer from practical, overlooked facility issues: failing audio amplifiers during Friday sermons, unlit prayer halls during power outages, inadequate Qur'an storage shelves, worn carpets requiring deep sanitation, or leaking roofs. While major mosque construction campaigns frequently receive public attention, small and moderate maintenance needs are often neglected.

At the same time, Muslims seeking avenues for Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing voluntary charity) want guaranteed assurance that their contributions reach genuine needs, are managed with zero waste, and produce documented, long-term spiritual benefit.

Our Governing Philosophy
"Small Acts. Lasting Benefit."

We do not operate as a major construction charity. We focus specifically on practical, high-impact interventions that immediately enhance the dignity, safety, and functionality of existing masajid.

The 9-Point Foundational Charter

Core organizational principles governing how needs are audited, funded, and reported transparently.

01. Genuine Need Assessment

Identify authentic, practical facility requirements within masajid through community nominations and physical audits.

02. Targeted Support

Focus on small to moderate improvement projects that create immediate, tangible benefit for worshippers.

03. Essential Resources

Supply high-quality Qur'ans, lighting, sound equipment, and wudhu fittings that directly support worship and learning.

04. Hygiene & Sanitation

Promote deep carpet cleaning, anti-bacterial washing, and clean wudhu facilities to uphold sacred cleanliness.

05. Facility Safety

Fix electrical hazards, roof leaks, slippery tiles, and security gaps to ensure a secure environment for worshippers.

06. Sadaqah Jariyah Access

Provide accessible, transparent opportunities for donors of all giving capacities to participate in ongoing charity.

07. Visual Evidence

Document every completed intervention with high-resolution before, during, and after photographic proof.

08. 100% Public Ledger

Publish all received donations and itemized contractor payments on an unalterable Transparency Dashboard.

09. Shared Responsibility

Foster collective community responsibility for the care, maintenance, and long-term upkeep of houses of Allah.

The 6-Stage Project Lifecycle

Our systematic methodology from community identification to 12-month post-completion monitoring.

STAGE 1

Identification

Need submitted via community nomination or field team discovery.

STAGE 2

Verification Audit

Physical site visit, safety audit, and initial feasibility assessment.

STAGE 3

Scope & Consent

Itemized technical budget approval and consent from mosque leadership.

STAGE 4

Crowdfunding

Project published with itemized scope budget and live donation progress.

STAGE 5

Execution

Materials purchased, work executed by licensed technicians, and receipts logged.

STAGE 6

12-Mo Monitoring

Check-ins conducted at 3, 6, and 12 months to verify ongoing performance.

Governance & Oversight

Independent trustees, financial auditors, and field operations teams ensuring total accountability.

Board of Trustees

Provides overarching fiduciary oversight, strategic direction, and ensures adherence to the core charter.

Advisory Council

Offers guidance on Sadaqah guidelines, project eligibility standards, and community engagement integrity.

Finance & Audit Function

Monitors donor receipts, verifies contractor invoices, and logs all disbursements on the Transparency Dashboard.

Field Operations Team

Conducts site audits, manages technical vendors, captures photographic proof, and executes monitoring check-ins.
