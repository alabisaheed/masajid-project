Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about contributing, project governance, Islamic compliance, and financial transparency.

Where does my donation go?
100% of your donation is directed toward materials and vendor labor for the specific project you select. Masajid Project operates on a 100% restricted fund policy. Zero platform fees are deducted from project contributions. All administrative costs are managed separately.
Can I give anonymously?
Yes. You may contribute without your name appearing anywhere on the public donation ledger. We only require your email address internally so we can send your payment receipt and project completion progress reports.
Can I dedicate a contribution in memory of someone?
Yes. During the donation flow, you may enter a dedication or Niyyah in memory of or on behalf of a parent, family member, or loved one. This intention is recorded privately with your contribution.
What currencies are accepted for giving?
We accept contributions in NGN (Nigerian Naira), GBP (£ British Pounds), and USD ($ US Dollars). All foreign currency gifts are converted transparently at prevailing exchange rates and logged on the project escrow record.
Will I receive a receipt for my payment?
Yes. An automated digital receipt containing your unique transaction reference code, project details, currency amount, and date is sent immediately to your email address upon payment confirmation.
Why does mosque maintenance qualify as Sadaqah Jariyah?
The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) taught that when a person passes away, their deeds cease except for three, including Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing charity). Providing lighting, sound amplification, carpets, or Qur'ans for a mosque yields continuous reward every time worshippers pray, read, or listen.
How do I submit or nominate a mosque for support?
Visit our "Support a Masjid" page and fill out the simple nomination form. Tell us about the mosque, location, and the specific practical need (lighting, sound, solar power, deep clean, Qur'ans). Our field team will review it within 24 hours.
Is there any cost or fee charged to the mosque?
No. Masajid Project does not charge masajid for technical assessments, vendor management, or project execution. All supported interventions are funded entirely through crowdsourced Sadaqah Jariyah.
What types of needs are eligible for support?
We focus on practical, moderate mosque needs: energy efficient LED lighting, solar power backup, sound system upgrades, carpet deep cleaning & sanitation, Qur'ans & book storage, security equipment, and minor facility repairs.
Who approves project execution at the mosque?
No work commences without formal written consent from the Imam and executive committee of the mosque. Our field team meets directly with mosque leadership before listing any project.
How long does a project take from nomination to completion?
Smaller interventions (lighting or carpet deep cleaning) are typically executed within 7 to 14 days after funding completion. Solar power or sound system upgrades take between 2 to 4 weeks.
What happens after the project is completed?
After execution, high-resolution before/after photographs and supplier invoices are published publicly. The project then enters a 12-month monitoring cycle where our field team conducts check-ins at 3, 6, and 12 months.
How can I verify how money was spent on a project?
Visit our Transparency Dashboard at any time. Every naira received and every naira disbursed to contractors is published with supplier business name, invoice date, bank transfer reference, and receipt proof.
Are cash payments given directly to mosque leadership?
No. To maintain complete financial integrity, Masajid Project never disburses unverified cash. Payments are transferred directly to verified material suppliers, electricians, and contractors upon presentation of audited invoices.
Who oversees the financial management of Masajid Project?
Financial management is overseen by our Finance & Audit Committee under the governance of the Board of Trustees. Quarterly financial audits are published publicly on our Transparency Dashboard.
Does Masajid Project accept Zakat funds?
No. Masajid Project accepts voluntary Sadaqah only and does not accept Zakat. Zakat has specific jurisprudence guidelines for eligible human recipients. Mosque physical infrastructure falls under Sadaqah Jariyah.
What happens if a project raises more than its required goal?
Any surplus funds are handled transparently: they are either retained for a follow-up maintenance reserve at the same mosque or transferred to the General Sadaqah Jariyah Fund to support the next urgent masjid need.
How are vendors and contractors selected?
Our procurement policy requires at least 3 competitive quotations for any technical procurement over ₦100,000. Vendors are evaluated on material quality, pricing, delivery speed, and local warranty commitments.
