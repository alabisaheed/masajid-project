1. Data Controller & Scope

Masajid Project Initiative acts as the primary data controller for all personal information collected through our platform, online forms, donation checkouts, and mosque submission channels. This policy governs how we collect, process, protect, and handle data provided by donors, mosque representatives, and platform visitors worldwide.

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We collect minimal, necessary data required to process contributions and audit mosque nominations:

  • Donor Data: Full name, email address, payment reference, and voluntary dedication notes (Niyyah).
  • Mosque Submission Data: Mosque name, city/location, contact person details, phone numbers, and physical audit notes.
  • Technical Logs: Device browser type, IP address, and session timestamps collected for security and fraud prevention.

3. Legal Basis for Data Processing

We process personal data based on explicit donor consent (when initiating a contribution), legitimate charitable mandate (issuing audited payment receipts and verifying mosque safety), and statutory legal obligations (financial auditing and anti-money laundering compliance).

4. Anonymous Giving Rights & Public Ledger Protocols

Supporters maintain absolute rights to give anonymously. When the anonymous checkbox is selected during checkout, your name is permanently suppressed from all public donation ledgers, project summary pages, and marketing communications. Internal transaction logs record only your anonymized reference code.

5. Payment Security & PCI-DSS Encryption

All card transactions and online bank transfers are processed directly through licensed PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Paystack/Flutterwave/Stripe). Masajid Project never stores, handles, or accesses raw credit card numbers or bank account PINs on our servers.

6. Data Security & Retention Schedules

Donor records and financial receipt histories are retained for 7 years to fulfill charity audit compliance. All stored records are protected using AES-256 bit encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

7. Third-Party Protections

We strictly forbid the selling, renting, trading, or commercial sharing of donor personal details with external advertisers or telemarketers. Personal details collected during mosque nominations are shared only with licensed local technical contractors assigned to that specific intervention.
