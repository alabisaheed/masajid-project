The Continuous Blessings of Placing Qur'ans and Reading Stands

AS
Alabi Saheed
20 Aug 2026 • 5 min read

Picture this simple scene: A young child walks into the local mosque after school, takes a copy of the Holy Qur'an from a neat wooden bookshelf, places it gently on a wooden stand (Rehal), and begins reciting with a pure heart.

"Whoever recites a single letter from the Book of Allah will receive one reward, and that reward is multiplied ten times. I do not say that Alif-Lam-Mim is one letter; Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter, and Mim is a letter." — Sunan al-Tirmidhi

Honoring the Word of Allah in Local Mosques

In many small neighborhood mosques, copies of the Holy Qur'an are kept on dusty floors or stacked precariously on window sills because there are no bookshelves. Elders struggle with back pain because they have to hold heavy books for hours without reading stands.

When you donate solid wooden bookshelves and comfortable reading stands, you restore honor to the sacred text. You make reciting and memorizing the Qur'an a joyful, dignified experience for everyone.

That young student will recite Surah Al-Fatihah thousands of times throughout their life. Every single prayer they perform, and every verse they teach their own children decades later, sends continuous reward back to the person who provided that bookshelf and stand. That is the true miracle of Sadaqah Jariyah.
